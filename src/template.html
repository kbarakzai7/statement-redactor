Statement Redactor

Runs entirely in your browser. Files are never uploaded.

1. Add PDFs

Drop PDFs here or click to choose Credit card, bank and other statements

2. Names & words to always redact

Not case sensitive. Matches on every page of every file.

3. What to find automatically

Click any box on a page to turn it off or back on. Drag on a page to add your own box. Manual boxes are removed with a click.

Output quality

The redacted PDF is rebuilt from page images, so hidden text under the black boxes cannot be copied or recovered.

Detection is automatic and can miss things. Check every page before sharing.
Built with PDF.js (Apache-2.0) and pdf-lib (MIT).

Redact statements before you share them

Account numbers, card numbers, names, addresses, phone numbers, emails and SIN/SSN are found for you. You review, adjust, then download a clean copy.
1
Add one or more PDFs. Text-based statements downloaded from online banking work best.
2
Add your name and anything else personal in the box on the left, for full coverage.
3
Check every page. Turn off boxes you want visible, drag to add boxes the scan missed.
4
Download. The output is checked automatically to confirm no readable text survived.
